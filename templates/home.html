About Gregado

Gregado is a professional education consultancy platform designed to eliminate confusion and bring direction to your higher education journey. We provide expert guidance tailored to each student's goals, helping you choose the right course, college, and destination with absolute clarity.

✦ Core Benefits

  • 100% Student-Focused 1:1 Career Guidance.
  • Direct access to top institutions across 10+ South Indian destinations.
  • Complete transparency on fee structures and syllabus (Downloadable PDFs).
  • Hassle-free admission support and global placement tie-ups.
